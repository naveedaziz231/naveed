Manufacture Philosophy

The Sacred Geometry of Mechanical Mastery

Nestled within the snow-covered valleys of Le Brassus, the Aurelius atelier embodies over a century of unrelenting dedication to mechanical horology. Unlike industrial mass production, our craft begins where machines reach their limitations.

Each balance wheel, pinion, and hand-chamfered bridge is shaped by master artisans whose lineage spans generations. It takes upwards of 400 hours of devoted handcrafting to bring a single Aurelius movement to life.

Flying Tourbillon & Complications
