Historical Milestones

A Chronology of Innovation

Key moments that defined the Aurelius aesthetic and mechanical legacy.

1888

Founding in Le Brassus

Antoine Aurelius establishes the first independent horological workshop in the high cradle of Swiss watchmaking, dedicated exclusively to pocket chronometers with minute repeaters.

1934

The First Flying Tourbillon

Pioneering the cantilevered tourbillon bridge, freeing the escapement cage from upper support for unmatched visual clarity and gravity compensation.

PRESENT

The Modern Renaissance

Combining classical Grand Feu enamelling and hand-turned guilloché with cutting-edge tantalum metallurgies and bespoke collector commissions.

Values & Creed

The Four Tenets of Aurelius

100% In-House Manufacture

From initial caliber blueprints to final regulation, every single component is designed, machined, hand-finished, and assembled in our Swiss ateliers.

Poinçon de Genève Standards

Every bridge is chamfered with anglage, screw heads mirror-polished, and gear teeth polished with diamond paste for friction-free longevity.

Restoration in Perpetuity

We maintain archival blueprints and tooling to restore, service, or recreate parts for any Aurelius timepiece produced since 1888.
