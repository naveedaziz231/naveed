Master Complications

The Mechanical Wonders

Each complication engineered, adjusted, and hand-finished to supreme chronometric standards.

I

Astronomical Moonphase

Accurate to one single lunar day deviation every 122.6 years, our moonphase discs are laser-etched on solid gold and set in midnight blue aventurine glass.

II

Cathedral Minute Repeater

Tuned by master acoustic artisans, the circular gongs wrap twice around the caliber to project crystal-clear harmonic chimes for hours, quarters, and minutes.

III

Perpetual Secular Calendar

A mechanical memory calculating leap years, centuries, and non-leap century years (2100, 2200, 2300) without requiring manual correction for over four centuries.

The Bespoke Commissioning Journey

From initial private consultation to final hand-delivery in our Geneva salon.

01. Private Salon Consultation

Meet with our Master Horologist in Geneva, London, or via encrypted video concierge to define your technical requirements, complication wishes, and metallurgical preferences.

02. Hand-Drawn Blueprints

Delivered in a handcrafted Swiss walnut presentation box with permanent archival documentation, VIP passport, and bespoke warranty.
